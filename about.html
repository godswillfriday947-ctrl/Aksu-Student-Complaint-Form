ABOUT THE SYSTEM

AKWA IBOM STATE UNIVERSITY Student Complaint Management System

AKWA IBOM STATE UNIVERSITY Student Complaint Management System is a platform designed to make it easier for students to submit their Complaints and monitor the progress of their personal dashboard.

University administrators can review submitted Complaints, manage their status and accesss the information needed to resolve student issues.

What The System Provides

A simple and organized way to handle student Complaints

Easy Complaint Submission

Students can monitor the status of their submitted Complaints

Admin Management

Administrators can view and manage student complaints effeciently.
